Frequently asked questions

I got a new phone. How do I keep my progress?
On your old phone open Settings → Cloud save and note your recovery code (CF-XXXXX-XXXXXX). On the new phone install Color Flow, open Settings → Cloud save, enter the code and tap Load.

I bought "Remove ads" but I still see ads.
Open Shop → Restore purchases. Rewarded ads you choose to watch (for hints or extra bottles) stay available.

How do I add a friend?
Tap Friends on the home screen and share your friend code, or enter your friend's code (CF-XXXXX) and tap Add.

How do I delete my data?
Email us your friend code and we will delete your profile and cloud backup. See the Privacy Policy.
